1Introduction & Background

Project Overview

This project tackled the challenge of cleaning and consolidating a massive dataset — over 9,000 raw, multi-source business records — that combined sales and expense data from multiple feeds into a single export.

  • Inconsistent date formats that prevented accurate time-series analysis
  • Blank values making owener in confusion wheter to take it or not
  • Unverified business and expense records that introduced uncertainty into financial reporting
  • Unstructured product and expense categories that made classification and aggregation unreliable

The objective was to engineer a clean, analysis-ready format that downstream dashboards could safely consume.

3Insights & Analysis

Key Findings & Data Quality Improvements

The standardization process exposed critical data quality issues that would have introduced significant margin errors in downstream financial models.

  • Identified 4,217 duplicate SKU entries across three warehouse feeds.
  • Detected 3 conflicting date formats unified safely to ISO 8601.
  • Currency normalization reduced a 12% revenue discrepancy.
  • Taxonomy re-mapping collapsed 47 raw labels down to 8 standardized parent categories.
